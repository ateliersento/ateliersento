The Doll Shop

An indie game Coming in 2026

Part romance, part horror, The Doll Shop is the tale of a quiet, secretive dollmaker living in the Japanese countryside. When his childhood friend returns to the village, he must choose between his beloved dolls and the hope of a new life… if it’s not already too late.

First released as a free prototype made during a masterclass with students, The Doll Shop has been entirely rebuilt with new artwork, music, and dialogue.

FESTIVAL OF SHADOWS

A graphic novel available at Tuttle publishing

In a Japanese village, the inhabitants are entrusted to assist the "shadows" of those who died tragically to find peace. Naoko, a dreamy young woman, is given a year to save the soul of a mysterious man. As the years goes by, Naoko finds herself teetering between the worlds of the living and the dead. Could she be risking her own life to help someone who has already lost his?
