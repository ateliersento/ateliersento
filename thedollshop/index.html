The Doll Shop is a hand-drawn horrific narrative game with a touch of romance , set in the Japanese countryside. Play as a young dollmaker who reunites with his childhood friend in an eerie snowbound village. Will you dare confess your secret? Depending on your choices, you'll access different endings.


Part romance, part horror, The Doll Shop is the tale of a quiet, secretive dollmaker living in the Japanese countryside. When his childhood friend returns to the village, he must choose between his beloved dolls and the hope of a new life . . . if it’s not already too late.

  • 3 different endings - Find them all to unlock a hidden one!
  • A Blend of Adventure Game and Visual Novel - Freely explore the village, enter homes and shops, and talk to the locals. But choose your words carefully – they may have consequences.
  • An Emotional Journey The Doll Shop focuses on the emotional states of its characters. There’s a strange comfort in its horror and twisted charm in its romance.
  • A Unique Art Style Every detail of the game is hand-drawn. We were inspired by the snowy landscapes of northern Japan, where we used to live.
  • Repair creepy dolls and reunite them with their owners.
  • Explore a small Japanese village: visit the shrine, soak in the public bath, and more.
  • Taste every ramen recipe at the local restaurant.
  • A Complete Remake First released as a free prototype made during a masterclass with students, The Doll Shop has been entirely rebuilt with new artwork, music, and dialogue.

We are Cecile and Olivier, a French couple working as graphic novel artists (ONIBI , Festival of Shadows , Tokyo Mystery Cafe) inspired by our travels in Japan. In 2009, thanks to a university exchange (Cecile was studying Japanese in Bordeaux), we have spent a whole year in the very cold countryside area of Niigata. This experience of a remote life during a long winter inspired us the story of The Doll Shop. All the watercolor sketches we made in Japan became our main documentation for this project. We also met an antique lover who revealed his collection of old Japanese items (especially dolls) to us. The numerous pictures we took served us as references to draw details and backgrounds of the game.


In 2018, the game creator Hideo Kojima found our graphic novel ONIBI (published in Japan) in a Tokyo bookstore. and started to follow our work. In 2023, for the documentation of our graphic novel series Tokyo Mystery Café, we visited the Kojima productions work space. We were scanned and became charatcters inside Death Stranding 2! Then we created a series of comic strips set inside DS2 world published on Instagram.
